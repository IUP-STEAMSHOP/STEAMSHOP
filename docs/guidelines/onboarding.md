# Onboarding

Welcome to the IUP Steamshop! This guide walks you through everything required before your first independent session in the lab.

---

## Step 1 — Attend Orientation

All new users must complete a **mandatory orientation session** before accessing equipment unsupervised. During orientation you will:

- Tour the facility and learn where tools and materials are stored
- Review emergency procedures and exit routes
- Meet the shop managers and learn how to request help

Contact a shop manager to schedule your orientation session.

---

## Step 2 — Read and Sign the Waiver

Before using any equipment you must read and sign the **IUP Steamshop User Agreement and Liability Waiver**. This document is available at the front desk on your first visit.

The waiver covers:

- Acknowledgement of general shop hazards
- Responsibility for safe equipment use
- Agreement to follow all posted SOPs and manager instructions

---

## Step 3 — Complete Safety Training

Read the [Safety Guidelines](safety.md) and the [Code of Conduct](conduct.md) in full. You may be asked questions about these during your orientation.

!!! warning "You cannot use equipment without completing this step."

---

## Step 4 — Get Machine-Specific Training

Access to most machines requires **individual training** with a shop manager before solo use is permitted. Machines are grouped by required training level:

| Training Level | Examples |
|---|---|
| General orientation only | Basic hand tools, vinyl cutter |
| Individual training required | 3D printers, laser cutters, drill press |
| Advanced certification required | CNC mills, lathes, welders, robotic arms |

Ask a shop manager to schedule training for any machine you want to use.

---

## Step 5 — Book Your Session

Once trained, use the shop scheduling system to book your workspace. Walk-in access is available during open lab hours, but booked sessions take priority.

---

## Contact & Hours

Reach out to the DigiLab team or check the front-desk calendar for current open lab hours and manager availability.
