# Code of Conduct

The IUP Steamshop is a shared professional workspace. Everyone — students, staff, faculty, and guests — is expected to behave in a way that keeps the environment safe, welcoming, and productive for all.

---

## Our Standards

We expect all Steamshop members to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat everyone in the space with courtesy and professionalism regardless of skill level or background.
- **Be responsible.** Take ownership of your workspace, your equipment usage, and your mistakes.
- **Be communicative.** Ask for help when you need it. Offer help when you can. Speak up if something is wrong.
- **Follow instructions.** Comply with shop manager directions and all posted operating procedures.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Deliberately damaging or misusing equipment
- Removing tools or materials from the shop without authorization
- Using equipment without proper training or authorization
- Interfering with another person's active project or session
- Ignoring safety rules or manager instructions

---

## Equipment Etiquette

- **Book your time.** If a machine is in use, wait your turn or book a session.
- **Leave it better than you found it.** Clean machines, clear surfaces, and return all tools after use.
- **Report damage.** If you accidentally damage equipment or cause an error, tell a manager immediately. Hiding it makes things worse.
- **Don't modify settings** on shared machines without manager approval (e.g., default slicer profiles, machine calibration).

---

## Consequences

Violations of this code may result in:

1. A verbal warning from a shop manager
2. Temporary suspension of shop access
3. Permanent revocation of access privileges
4. Referral to university student conduct or HR processes (depending on severity)

---

## Questions or Concerns

If you have concerns about the conduct of another person in the shop, or if you have experienced or witnessed harassment, report it to a shop manager or the DigiLab administration. All reports are taken seriously.
