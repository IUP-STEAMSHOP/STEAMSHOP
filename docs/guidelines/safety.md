# Safety Guidelines

Safety is the Steamshop's top priority. These rules apply to every person in the facility at all times — no exceptions.

---

## General Rules

!!! danger "Violations of safety rules may result in immediate removal from the shop and loss of access privileges."

- **Never use a machine you have not been trained on.** Each SOP page lists the required training level.
- **Always wear appropriate PPE.** Safety glasses are required on the shop floor at all times. Hearing protection and gloves are required for specific machines — check the individual SOP.
- **No food or drink** in the fabrication areas.
- **No loose clothing, dangling jewelry, or unsecured long hair** near rotating or moving machinery.
- **Never leave a running machine unattended.**
- **Report all malfunctions immediately** to a shop manager. Do not attempt to repair equipment yourself.
- **Clean up your workspace** before leaving. Return all tools to their designated locations.

---

## Emergency Procedures

### Fire
1. Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station.
2. Evacuate through the nearest marked exit.
3. Do not use elevators.
4. Assemble at the designated meeting point outside.
5. Do not re-enter until cleared by emergency personnel.

### Injury
1. Stop all work immediately.
2. Alert a shop manager.
3. Call campus emergency services if needed.
4. First-aid kits are located at the front desk and near each major work area.

### Chemical Spill / Fume Release
1. Evacuate the immediate area.
2. Alert a shop manager.
3. Do not attempt to clean up chemical spills yourself.

## Machine-Specific Hazards

| Category | Key Hazard | Required PPE |
|---|---|---|
| 3D Printers | Hot end burns, fumes (ABS/ASA) | Ventilation required for ABS/ASA |
| Laser Cutters | Laser radiation, fumes, fire | Safety glasses, active ventilation |
| CNC / Mills | Flying chips, rotating spindle | Safety glasses, no gloves near spindle |
| Woodworking | Kickback, blade contact, dust | Safety glasses, hearing protection, dust mask |
| Metalworking | Sharp edges, heat, metal chips | Safety glasses, gloves (when handling stock only) |
| Welding | Arc flash, UV radiation, fumes | Welding helmet, gloves, ventilation |
| Robotics | Unexpected arm movement | Stay behind safety boundary during operation |

---

## Reporting Issues

If you observe an unsafe condition — a damaged machine, missing guard, or hazardous material — report it to a shop manager immediately. Do not wait for someone else to report it.
