## How to Submit Your Project

To have your project listed on this page:

1. Speak with a shop manager or DigiLab staff member
2. Provide your project name, a one-sentence description, the machines you plan to use, and your expected completion date
3. Your entry will be added to this page and tracked through completion

Completed projects are moved to the [Past Projects](../archive/completed.md) archive.
