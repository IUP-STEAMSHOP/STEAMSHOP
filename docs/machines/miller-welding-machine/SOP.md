## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

PPE Mandatory: Always wear a welding helmet (correct shade), flame-resistant gloves, and leather boots.  
Ventilation: Ensure the exhaust fan is ON; never weld in a confined space without proper airflow.  
Fire Safety: Clear all flammable materials, including rags and solvents, from the welding area before starting.  
Dry Conditions: Never weld in damp or wet conditions; ensure the ground clamp is attached directly to clean metal.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Connect the ground clamp to the workpiece or the welding table.  
Open the shielding gas cylinder valve slowly and check the regulator pressure.  
Switch the main power toggle to ON.  
Adjust the voltage and wire feed speed (MIG) or amperage (TIG/Stick) based on the material thickness.  

### Shut-Down Sequence:

Turn OFF the power switch.  
Close the gas cylinder valve completely.  
Trigger the torch to bleed the remaining gas from the lines until the regulator gauge reads zero.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Porous Welds: Check if the gas cylinder is empty or if there is a draft blowing the shielding gas away from the puddle.  
Wire Stops Feeding: Check for a bird's nest at the drive rolls or a clogged contact tip.  
Weak Arc: Ensure the ground clamp is attached to a clean, unpainted surface.
