## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Eye Protection: Always wear safety glasses or a face shield when operating the drill press.  
Entanglement Hazard: Tie back long hair, remove loose clothing, and avoid dangling jewelry.  
No Gloves: Do not wear gloves while operating the spindle, as they can be caught in the rotating drill bit.  
Secure Workpiece: Always use a drill press vise or clamps to secure your material; never hold a workpiece by hand while drilling.  
Chuck Key: Never leave the chuck key in the chuck; remove it immediately after tightening or loosening a bit.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Adjust the table height and lock it in place.  
Insert the drill bit into the chuck and tighten it securely with the chuck key.  
Ensure the belt speed is set correctly for the material and bit size (refer to the speed chart inside the belt cover).  
Switch the power to ON.  

### Drilling:

Mark your hole location with a center punch to prevent bit "walk."  
Slowly lower the spindle using the feed handles until the bit contacts the material.  
Apply consistent pressure, backing the bit out frequently (peck drilling) to clear chips and prevent overheating.  
Turn the machine OFF and wait for the spindle to stop completely before removing the workpiece.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Machine Will Not Start: Check that the plug is secure and the emergency stop (if equipped) is released.  
Bit Slipping: Ensure the chuck is tightened properly using all three holes on the chuck body.  
Excessive Noise/Vibration: Check that the belt tension is correct and the bit is not bent or dull.  
Smoke or Burning: Reduce spindle speed, check focus on the cut, or use a sharper bit.
