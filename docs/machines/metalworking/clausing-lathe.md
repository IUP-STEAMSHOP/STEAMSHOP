## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

No Gloves: Never wear gloves while operating the lathe, as they can be easily caught in the rotating chuck or workpiece.  
Entanglement Hazard: Tie back long hair, remove loose clothing, and avoid dangling jewelry.  
Eye Protection: Always wear safety glasses or a face shield; flying metal chips are hot and sharp.  
Chuck Key: Never leave the chuck key in the chuck; remove it immediately after tightening or loosening.  
Clearance: Ensure the tool post and carriage have sufficient clearance before starting the spindle to prevent a crash.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Check that the spindle speed is set correctly for the material and diameter being turned.  
Ensure the gear levers are fully engaged and not in a neutral or "between" position.  
Ensure the Emergency Stop is released.  
Switch the main power to ON and engage the spindle lever.  

### Operational Steps:

Mounting: Secure the workpiece firmly in the chuck or between centers.  
Tool Setup: Set the cutting tool height exactly to the center line of the spindle.  
Manual Jogging: Use the handwheels to move the carriage and cross-slide to the starting position.  
Cutting: Engage the power feed only after confirming the feed rate and direction on the quick-change gearbox.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Machine Will Not Start: Verify the foot brake is not engaged and the chuck guard (if equipped) is fully closed.  
Excessive Chatter: Reduce the spindle speed, increase the feed rate, or check that the tool overhang is minimized.  
Poor Surface Finish: Check for a dull cutting edge or ensure the tool is positioned exactly at center height.
