## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Chemical Safety: Resin is a skin sensitizer. Always wear nitrile gloves and safety glasses when handling resin tanks, build platforms, or uncured prints.  
Ventilation: Operate in a well-ventilated area to minimize exposure to resin vapors.  
UV Exposure: Keep the printer cover closed during operation to prevent accidental UV curing of resin in the tank.  
Spill Response: If resin is spilled, clean immediately with Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) and paper towels.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Plug in the printer and wait for the touchscreen to initialize.  
Ensure the resin tank is properly seated and the mixer is attached.  
Insert a resin cartridge and open the vent cap.  

### Printing:

Prepare the file in PreForm software, selecting the correct material and layer thickness.  
Send the job to the printer via Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or USB.  
On the printer's touchscreen, select the job and press Print.  
Confirm the build platform and resin tank match the file requirements.  

## POST-PROCESSING

Remove the build platform and place it in the Form Wash (IPA bath) for the specified time.  
Once dry, remove supports carefully using snips.  
Place the part in the Form Cure to reach final mechanical properties.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Printer Will Not Start or Screen is Blank  
Check Power Connection: Ensure the power cable is firmly seated in the back of the printer and the outlet.  
Power Cycle: Turn the printer off using the switch at the back, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on.  
Firmware Hang: If the screen is frozen, unplug the machine for one minute and restart. Ensure the printer is connected to Wi-Fi or Ethernet to check for mandatory firmware updates.  
Print Fails to Adhere to Build Platform  
Clean the Platform: Ensure the aluminum build surface is completely free of cured resin or oil. Clean with 90% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA).  
Check Resin Level: Verify the cartridge is not empty and the bite valve is not stuck shut, preventing resin from filling the tank.  
Tank Inspection: Check the bottom of the resin tank (the film) for "ghosting" or cured debris. Debris on the film will block the LFS laser and prevent the first layer from forming.  
? Resin Not Filling Tank  
Bite Valve Check: Inspect the rubber bite valve at the bottom of the resin cartridge. If it is stuck, gently squeeze it with gloved fingers to loosen the seal.  
Air Vent: Ensure the vent cap on top of the resin cartridge is OPEN during printing to allow for proper flow.  
Cartridge Chip: Ensure the ID chip on the bottom of the cartridge is clean and making contact with the printer's reader.  
"Missing Tank" or "Missing Cartridge" Error  
Clean Contacts: Use a cotton swab dampened with IPA to gently clean the small gold spring contacts on the printer and the corresponding chips on the tank/cartridge.  
Re-seat Hardware: Remove the tank or cartridge and slide it back in firmly until it clicks or the printer acknowledges it.  
Debris in Resin Tank (Cured Flakes)  
Filter the Resin: If a print fails, there may be small bits of cured plastic floating in the liquid. Use a fine mesh paint filter to strain the resin before starting a new job.  
Cleaning Mesh: Use the "Cleaning Mesh" feature on the printer's maintenance menu to create a thin cured sheet that pulls all debris off the bottom of the tank at once.  
When in Doubt  
Wear Gloves: Always wear nitrile gloves when handling resin or internal components.  
Protect the Glass: Never touch the optical window (the glass under the tank) with bare fingers. If it is dirty, use only PEC*PADs and 99% IPA.  
No Scrapers: Never use a metal scraper on the flexible tank film; use only the provided plastic tank tool.  
Ask for help: SLA printing involves hazardous chemicals; if a spill occurs inside the printer, notify a supervisor immediately.
