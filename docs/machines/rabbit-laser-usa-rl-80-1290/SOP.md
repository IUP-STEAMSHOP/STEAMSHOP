## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Supervision: NEVER leave the laser unattended while running.  
Observation: Always remain in the room and watch the job from start to finish.  
Personal Safety: Tie back long hair, remove loose clothing, and avoid dangling jewelry.  
Safety Features: Do not bypass or disable safety features.  
Fire Preparedness: Laser cutting can ignite materials; be prepared at all times.  
Materials NOT Allowed: Never cut or engrave PVC/Vinyl, Polycarbonate, ABS, Fiberglass, or unknown foams.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Turn ON water chiller.  
Turn ON exhaust fan.  
Turn ON air assist.  
Turn ON main laser power.  
Power on the Ruida control panel and wait for system stabilization.  

### Focal Length Setup:

Place material flat on the laser bed.  
Move laser head over material.  
On control panel, select Auto Focus / Auto Adjust.  
If manual focus is used, place focus gauge between nozzle and material and raise bed until it fits snugly.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Laser Will Not Fire: Check that the lid is fully closed, the water chiller is running, and the emergency stop is released.  
Cuts Not Going Through: Check focus, clean lens and mirrors, or increase power/slow speed.  
Water Flow Alarm: Check water level and ensure hoses are not kinked; do NOT bypass the alarm.  
Smoke Not Clearing: Verify the exhaust fan is ON and ducting is unobstructed
