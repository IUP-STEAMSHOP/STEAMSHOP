## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

High Pressure Hazard: Never attempt to touch the water stream or place hands under the cutting head while the high-pressure pump is active.  
Eye Protection: Always wear safety glasses to protect against abrasive splashback and water spray.  
Water Levels: Ensure the tank is filled to the correct level before starting to prevent pump damage.  
Abrasive Handling: Use only approved garnet abrasive; handle with care to avoid inhaling dust when filling the hopper.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Turn on the water supply valve.  
Switch the main power button to ON.  
Check the abrasive hopper level and refill if necessary.  
Clear the cutting bed of any old material or debris.  

### Running a Job:

Upload your design via the Wazer software (WAM).  
Secure your material using the provided clamps to prevent shifting.  
Ensure the nozzle height is set correctly using the provided feeler gauge.  
Press START on the control panel.  

## MAINTENANCE

Abrasive Disposal: Regularly empty the used abrasive collection buckets.  
Nozzle Wear: Inspect the mixing tube/nozzle for wear every 10-20 hours of cutting time.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

High-Pressure Pump Will Not Start  
Check Water Supply: Ensure the water source is turned ON and the inlet hose is not kinked. The pump will not engage without sufficient water pressure.  
Low Water Level: Check the main tank; if the water level sensor is not submerged, the machine will prevent the pump from starting to avoid damage.  
E-Stop & Power: Verify the Emergency Stop is released and the machine is connected to a dedicated 20A circuit.  
Software Interlock: Ensure the WAM software has "Handshaked" with the machine and there are no active software warnings.  
Abrasive (Garnet) Is Not Flowing  
Check the Hopper: Ensure the abrasive hopper is at least 1/4 full.  
Moisture in Hopper: If the garnet is damp, it will "clump" and stop feeding. Empty the hopper and refill with dry, sifted abrasive.  
Clogged Orifice: Inspect the abrasive feed line for obstructions. If the nozzle has a "backflow" (water entering the abrasive line), the orifice is likely clogged.  
Pinch Valve: Verify the internal pinch valve is operating correctly and not stuck in the closed position.  
Poor Cut Quality or Nozzle Dragging  
Nozzle Height: Ensure the nozzle is set exactly 1.5mm to 2mm above the material using the provided height gauge. If it is too high, the kerf will be wide and tapered.  
Worn Mixing Tube: Mixing tubes are consumables; if the exit hole is no longer perfectly round or has become significantly larger, replace the tube.  
Material Shifting: Ensure the material is clamped securely to the bed. The pressure of the water jet can move unsecured sheets, causing ruined cuts.  
Incorrect Material Settings: Verify that the correct material type and thickness are selected in the WAM software.  
Tank Overflowing or Drainage Issues  
Abrasive Collection: Check the two abrasive collection buckets. If they are full of used garnet, water cannot drain properly and will overflow the tank.  
Drain Filter: Inspect the internal filters and drain screens for debris or heavy sediment buildup.  
Pump Cycles: If the tank isn't draining, ensure the internal circulation pump is functioning correctly.  
When in Doubt  
STOP the machine: If the water jet cuts through the bed or sprays erratically, hit the E-Stop immediately.  
Dry the Area: The Wazer uses high-pressure water; if leaks occur, power down the machine before touching any electrical components.  
Abrasive Disposal: Never pour used garnet down a standard sink drain; it will cause a permanent clog.  
Ask for help: Waterjets are complex; if the pump sounds like it is "cavitating" (loud, irregular popping), consult a supervisor.
