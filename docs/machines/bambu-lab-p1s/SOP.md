---


## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Ventilation: Ensure the internal fan and room ventilation are active, especially when printing ABS or ASA.  
Automated Motion: The toolhead moves at extremely high speeds. Keep the front door and top glass closed during operation.  
Lid Safety: Opening the door does not automatically stop the print; use the "Pause" command on the screen or app.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Flip the power switch on the rear of the machine.  
The screen will light up and the machine will perform a homing routine.  

### Uploading Files:

Use Bambu Studio to slice your files.  
Send the file via Wi-Fi (Cloud) or save to a MicroSD card.  
Select "Print" from the interface or the Bambu Handy app.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Filament Jam: If the AMS (Automatic Material System) fails to load, check for brittle filament or broken pieces in the PTFE tubes.  
First Layer Issues: Ensure you have selected the correct build plate type (Textured PEI vs. Cool Plate) in the slicer.
