## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Hand Safety: Keep hands at least 6 inches away from the blade path. Never cross your arms when holding the material.  
Eye Protection: Safety glasses are always required.  
Material Support: Always use the fence. For long pieces, use external stands to prevent the material from tipping or binding.  
Wait for Stop: Never lift the saw head until the blade has come to a complete and total stop.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Ensure the blade guard moves freely.  
Plug in the saw and turn on the integrated work light/laser line if equipped.  
Squeeze the trigger to start the motor. Allow the blade to reach full speed before contacting the wood.  

### Adjusting Angles:

Miter: Unlock the miter handle and move the arm to the desired degree (0°, 22.5°, 45°).  
Bevel: Release the bevel lock lever at the rear of the saw to tilt the head for angled vertical cuts.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Inaccurate Cuts: Check for sawdust buildup against the fence or under the miter plate.  
Motor bogs down: Ensure you are not forcing the cut too quickly; let the speed of the blade do the work.
