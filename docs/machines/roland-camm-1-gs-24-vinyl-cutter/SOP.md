## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Entanglement: Keep hands and lose clothing away from the moving carriage and blade.  
Power Safety: Do not operate if the machine emits smoke, burning odors, or unusual noises.  
Blade Safety: Do not touch the tip of the blade; it is extremely sharp.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Setup:

Blade Height: Adjust the blade holder so the tip protrudes roughly with the thickness of a credit card.  
Load Material: Lift pinch rollers, insert vinyl (roll or piece), and align with the marks.  
Set Origin: Use arrow keys to move the blade to the starting point and press the [Origin] button.  

### Software:

Import vector data (DXF, SVG, or AI) into Roland CutStudio.  
Perform a Test Cut to ensure the blade force (typically ~90 gf) is correct for your material.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Machine Has Power but Won't Move  
Check Loading Lever: Ensure the loading lever is fully raised to the "Secure" position; the carriage will not move if the material is not locked.  
Pinch Roller Alignment: Verify that the pinch rollers are positioned directly over the grit rollers (indicated by white marks); the machine will error if they are misaligned.  
Clear Obstructions: Check for scrap vinyl or debris stuck in the carriage path or under the cutting strip.  
Unusual Noise: If the machine emits unusual noises or burning odors, power down immediately and unplug the unit.  
Vinyl is Slipping or Feeding Crooked  
Check Alignment: Ensure the vinyl is loaded straight and aligned with the guide lines on the front and back of the machine.  
Roland Roller Tension: Verify that the pinch rollers are clean and not covered in adhesive residue.  
Material Size: Ensure the material width is sufficient for the job; use the [Menu] key to re-measure the loaded piece if necessary.  
Cuts Are Incomplete or Jagged  
Check Blade Force: If the blade is not cutting through the vinyl, increase the force setting (gf) on the control panel or in the software.  
Inspect Blade Tip: Check the blade for chips or broken tips; do not touch the tip directly as it is extremely sharp.  
Blade Extension: Ensure the blade is protruding roughly the thickness of a credit card from the holder.  
Protection Strip: Inspect the black Teflon protection strip under the blade path; if it is deeply grooved or scratched, it must be replaced to ensure even cutting pressure.  
Software Communication Error  
Check USB Connection: Ensure the USB cable is securely connected to both the cutter and a high-speed port on the PC.  
Correct Driver: Verify that the "Roland GS-24" is selected as the active printer/cutter in CutStudio or your design software.  
Set Origin: If the machine starts cutting in the wrong place, ensure you have set the [Origin] point on the machine's control panel before sending the file.  
When in Doubt  
STOP the machine: Turn off the power switch if the carriage behaves erratically.  
Power Cycle: Turn off the machine, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on to reset the internal memory.  
Ask a supervisor: If you are unsure about blade depth or force settings, consult a trained operator to avoid damaging the protection strip.  
Never "test" fixes: Do not reach toward the carriage while the machine is actively cutting
