## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Heat Hazard: The heating platen can reach temperatures exceeding 200°C; avoid all skin contact with the heated surfaces to prevent severe burns.  
Pinch Hazard: Keep hands and fingers clear of the hinge and clamping mechanism when closing the press.  
Fumes: Ensure adequate ventilation when pressing synthetic fabrics or vinyl that may release odors when heated.  
Electrical: Do not operate with a damaged power cord or in damp environments.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Plug the unit into a grounded outlet and switch the power to ON.  
Set the desired Temperature and Time using the digital control panel.  
Wait for the machine to reach the target temperature before use.  

### Pressing Procedure:

Position the garment or cap on the lower platen/form.  
Place the transfer or vinyl accurately on the material.  
Lower the handle and lock the press into place.  
When the timer sounds, carefully lift the handle and remove the material.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Inconsistent Transfers: Check that the pressure adjustment knob is set correctly for the thickness of the material.  
Machine Not Heating: Ensure the emergency stop (if equipped) is not engaged and the fuse/breaker has not tripped.  
Material Scorching: Reduce the temperature or the pressing time.
