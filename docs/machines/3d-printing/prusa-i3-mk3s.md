## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Heat Hazard: The nozzle can reach temperatures up to 280°C and the heatbed up to 120°C; do not touch during or immediately after operation.  
Moving Parts: Keep hands, hair, and loose clothing away from the X, Y, and Z-axis motors and belts while the printer is active.  
Pinch Points: Avoid reaching into the frame while the printer is homing or printing.  
Ventilation: Ensure the printer is in a well-ventilated area, especially when printing materials like ABS or ASA.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Check that the removable magnetic steel sheet is clean and properly aligned on the heatbed.  
Switch the power button (located on the side of the power supply) to ON.  
Wait for the LCD screen to display the main menu.  

### Loading Filament:

Select Preheat on the LCD menu and choose the material type (e.g., PLA, PETG).  
Once the nozzle reaches the target temperature, select Load Filament.  
Insert the filament into the extruder intake and follow the on-screen prompts until color flows from the nozzle.  

### Starting a Print:

Insert the SD card containing your .gcode file (prepared in PrusaSlicer).  
Select Print from SD on the menu.  
Choose your file and press the control knob to begin.  
Watch the First Layer Calibration to ensure the plastic adheres properly to the bed.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Poor Adhesion: Clean the steel sheet with 90%+ Isopropyl Alcohol to remove oils; avoid touching the print surface with bare fingers.  
First Layer Issues: If the nozzle is too high or too low, use the Live Adjust Z setting during the first layer to fine-tune the height.  
Thermal Error: Check that the room is not too cold and that the printer is away from direct drafts.
