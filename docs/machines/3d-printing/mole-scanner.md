## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Eye Safety: Avoid looking directly into the scanner’s light source for extended periods during operation.  
Cable Management: Ensure the connection cables are positioned to prevent tripping or accidental pulling of the scanner or connected PC.  
Handling: The scanner is a precision optical instrument; avoid dropping or subjecting it to heavy vibrations.  
Environmental Conditions: Operate in a dry environment away from direct, harsh sunlight which can interfere with the infrared sensors.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Setup:

Connect the Mole V2.2 to your computer using the provided USB and power cables.  
Launch the JMStudio software.  
Ensure the scanner is recognized by the software (indicated by a live preview window).  

### Scanning Modes:


### Turntable Mode:

Place the object on the center of the turntable.  
Select Turntable Mode in JMStudio.  
Click Initial to scan the background, then place the object and click Scan.  

### Handheld Mode:

Select Handheld Mode in the software.  
Maintain a distance of 15cm to 50cm from the object.  
Slowly move the scanner around the subject, ensuring the "distance indicator" stays in the green zone.  

### Post-Processing:

Once the scan is complete, click Stop.  
Use the Process button to align multiple scans and generate a point cloud.  
Export the final model as an .STL or .OBJ for 3D printing or CAD use.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Tracking Lost: If the software loses the object's position, move the scanner back to a previously scanned area with distinct features to regain tracking.  
Difficult Surfaces: For dark, shiny, or transparent objects, apply a light coat of 3D scanning spray or baby powder to create a matte surface.  
Alignment Issues: Use "Manual Alignment" in the software if the automatic process fails to merge two scan rotations correctly.
