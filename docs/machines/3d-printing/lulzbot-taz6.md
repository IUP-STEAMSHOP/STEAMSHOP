## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Heat Hazard: The hot end reaches 300°C and the bed reaches 120°C; do not touch until cooled.  
Moving Parts: Keep the print area clear of tools and hands while the gantry is moving.  
Automatic Cleaning: The printer performs a nozzle-wipe routine before every print; keep hands clear of the wiping pad area.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Turn on the power switch on the side of the control box.  
Clean the PEI print surface with Isopropyl Alcohol (unplugged/cool).  
Insert the SD card and select your file, or send the job via Cura LulzBot Edition.  
Observe the automatic bed leveling sequence to ensure the nozzle touches all four corners correctly.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Filament Stripping: Check for a clogged nozzle or ensure the idler tension on the extruder is set correctly.  
Bed Leveling Failure: Clean the nozzle tip of any residual plastic to ensure accurate electrical contact during the leveling routine.
