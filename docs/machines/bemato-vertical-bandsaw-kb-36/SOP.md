# BEMATO Vertical Bandsaw KB-36 Standard Operating Procedure

---


## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Blade Welder: Only trained personnel should use the integrated blade welder.  
Blade Tension: Check tension before every shift; do not over-tighten.  
Clearance: Keep the work table clear of off-cuts to prevent them from wedging against the blade.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Speed Range: Select the appropriate speed range (High/Low) based on the material type.  
Blade Guides: Position the guides as close to the work as possible to prevent blade deflection.  
Feeding: Use the miter gauge or a pusher for straight cuts in thick metal.  
Clean up: Use a brush to remove metal chips; never use your bare hands or compressed air near the guides.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Slow Cutting: Inspect blade teeth for dullness or missing teeth; replace if necessary.  
Coolant Failure: Check the reservoir level and ensure the nozzle is not clogged with chips.  
Squealing: Check the drive belt tension and inspect the pulley alignment.
