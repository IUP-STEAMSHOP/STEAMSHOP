Quick Reference Guide: OMTech AF3555-130W-CO2

SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

•	Supervision: NEVER leave the laser unattended while running; stay in the room and watch the job from start to finish.

•	Personal Safety: Tie back long hair, remove loose clothing, and avoid dangling jewelry.

•	Fire Safety: Laser cutting can ignite materials; be always prepared.

•	Emergency Action: If a small flame occurs, STOP the job and use a water spray bottle; for out-of-control fires, use an ABC fire extinguisher and evacuate if necessary.

•	Banned Materials: Never cut PVC/Vinyl (releases chlorine gas), Polycarbonate (Lexan), ABS plastic, Fiberglass, or unknown foams.

BASIC OPERATION

Power-On Sequence:

1\.	Turn ON water chiller.

2\.	Turn ON exhaust fan.

3\.	Turn ON air assist.

4\.	Turn ON main laser power.

5\.	Power on the Ruida control panel and wait for system stabilization.

Auto Adjust (Focal Length):

1\.	Place material flat on the bed.

2\.	Move laser head over the material.

3\.	Select Auto Focus / Auto Adjust on the control panel.

4\.	The machine will automatically set the correct focal distance.

TROUBLESHOOTING

•	Laser Will Not Fire: Check if the lid is fully closed, the water chiller is running with no alarms, and the E-stop is released.

•	Water Flow Alarm: Check water levels and ensure hoses are not kinked; do NOT bypass the alarm.

•	Smoke Not Clearing: Ensure the exhaust fan is ON and ducting is not blocked.
