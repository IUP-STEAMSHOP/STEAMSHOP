# Ultimaker S3 Dual-extrusion Standard Operating Procedure

📄 [Download Official Manufacturer Manual (DOCX)](manual.docx)

---


## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Dual Nozzles: Both nozzles can be hot simultaneously; use caution when working near the print head.  
Pinch Points: Do not reach into the printer while the gantry or build plate is in motion.  
Material Safety: Use only approved Ultimaker or compatible materials to prevent clogging and damage.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Power On: Switch the power button on the back of the machine.  
Load Material: Use the touchscreen to select "Material/PrintCore" and follow the prompts to load filament into Extruder 1 and/or Extruder 2.  
Calibration: The S3 uses active bed leveling; ensure the build plate is free of old plastic before starting.  
Printing: Insert a USB drive or send the job via Ultimaker Digital Factory.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

PrintCore Not Recognized: Ensure the PrintCore is pushed firmly into the print head and the latch is closed.  
Under-extrusion: Check for knots in the filament spool or a dirty feeder gear.  
Cloud Connection: Verify the printer is linked to your Ultimaker account for remote monitoring.
