## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Eye Protection: Always wear safety glasses or a face shield; the sander generates high-speed dust and debris.  
Entanglement: Do not wear gloves, loose clothing, or jewelry; tie back long hair.  
Grip: Never hold small workpieces by hand; use a miter gauge or a jig to maintain control.  
Work Direction: Always sand on the downward-moving side of the disc to prevent the workpiece from being lifted and thrown.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Check that the belt and disc are in good condition and not torn or excessively clogged.  
Adjust the work table to the desired angle and tighten the locking knobs.  
Turn ON the dust collection system.  
Switch the sander ON and allow it to reach full speed.  
Hold the workpiece firmly against the table and feed it lightly against the abrasive surface.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Belt Tracking: If the belt moves toward one side, use the tracking adjustment knob to center it while the machine is running.  
Excessive Heat: Do not force the material; use lighter pressure and allow the abrasive to do the work to prevent burning the wood or clogging the paper.
