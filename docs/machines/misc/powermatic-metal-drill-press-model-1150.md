## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Chuck Key: NEVER leave the chuck key in the chuck; remove it immediately after use.  
No Gloves: Do not wear gloves while operating a rotating spindle to prevent entanglement.  
Secure Work: Always clamp the workpiece or use a drill press vise; never hold metal by hand.  
Eye Protection: Safety glasses are mandatory due to high-speed metal chips.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Speed Selection: Adjust the variable speed dial ONLY while the motor is running.  
Tooling: Insert the drill bit and tighten securely using all three holes in the chuck.  
Table Setup: Position the table at the correct height and lock all clamping levers.  
Drilling: Apply steady pressure; use cutting oil for steel to prevent overheating.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Bit Slipping: Ensure the chuck is clean and the bit is tightened properly with the key.  
Smoke at Tip: Reduce spindle speed and apply more lubrication/cooling.  
Won't Start: Check the emergency stop and the main power disconnect.
