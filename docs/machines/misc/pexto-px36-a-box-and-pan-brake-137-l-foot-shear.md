## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

PPE Mandatory: Always wear safety glasses and heavy-duty work gloves when handling sheet metal to prevent lacerations from sharp edges.  
Capacity Limits: Never exceed the rated material thickness (typically 16 gauge mild steel); attempting to shear or bend thicker material can damage the blades or frame.  
Pinch Points: Keep fingers clear of the clamping bar and the cutting blade at all times.  
Foot Safety: Ensure your feet are clear of the foot treadle area before others operate the machine, and wear closed-toed shoes.  
BASIC OPERATION: PEXTO 137-L (Foot Shear)  
Alignment: Place the sheet metal on the bed and align it against the side gauge or back gauge for a square cut.  
Clamping: Ensure the material is flat against the bed to prevent "creeping" during the cut.  
Shearing: Place one foot firmly on the treadle and apply a quick, decisive downward force to shear the metal.  
Retrieval: Carefully remove the off-cut from the rear of the machine, being mindful of sharp burrs.  
BASIC OPERATION: PEXTO PX36-A (Box and Pan Brake)  
Finger Setup: For box folding, remove or rearrange the upper fingers to match the width of your workpiece.  
Clamping: Slide the metal under the fingers to your bend line and pull the clamping handle forward to lock the material in place.  
Bending: Lift the folding beam handles slowly to the desired angle, using the stop gauge for repeatable bends.  
Release: Push the clamping handle back to release the fingers and remove the part.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Inaccurate Bends: Check that all fingers are tightened securely and that the material is not slipping during clamping.  
Heavy Burrs on Shear: Inspect the blade for nicks or dullness; ensure the blade gap is adjusted correctly for the material thickness.  
Machine Sticking: Lightly lubricate moving joints and the blade faces with machine oil.
