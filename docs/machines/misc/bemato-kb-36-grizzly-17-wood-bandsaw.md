## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

Blade Guard Adjustment: Adjust the upper blade guide to be no more than 1/4" above the material being cut.  
PPE: Always wear safety glasses; hearing protection is recommended for extended use.  
Hand Safety: Use a push stick for small pieces to keep fingers away from the blade.  
Wait for Stop: Never back a piece of wood or metal out of a cut while the blade is moving.  

## BASIC OPERATION

Preparation: Check that the blade is properly tensioned and tracking in the center of the wheels.  
Guide Setup: Adjust the guide height for your specific workpiece thickness.  
Dust/Coolant: Turn ON the dust collection (wood) or coolant pump (metal/Bemato) if applicable.  
Cutting: Press the START button and feed material slowly, avoiding excessive side pressure.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Blade Wandering: Check blade tension and ensure the guide bearings are properly adjusted.  
Excessive Vibration: Stop the machine and check for a dull blade or debris on the wheels.  
Motor Bogs Down: Reduce the feed rate; do not force the material into the blade.
