## SAFETY FIRST (READ BEFORE USE)

PPE Mandatory: Always wear a welding helmet with the correct shade, flame-resistant gloves, and a leather apron.  
Ventilation: Ensure the exhaust system is ON or work in a well-ventilated area to avoid inhaling toxic fumes.  
Fire Hazard: Clear the area of all flammable materials (rags, paper, solvents) before striking an arc.  
Electrical Safety: Never weld in damp or wet conditions. Ensure the machine is properly grounded.  

## BASIC OPERATION


### Power-On Sequence:

Connect the ground clamp to the workpiece or welding table.  
Open the valve on the shielding gas cylinder (if using MIG/TIG).  
Switch the main power toggle to ON.  
Adjust voltage and wire feed speed (or amperage) based on the material thickness chart found inside the side panel.  

### Shut-Down Sequence:

Turn OFF the welding machine power.  
Close the shielding gas cylinder valve completely.  
Trigger the gun (MIG) to bleed the remaining gas from the lines.  

## TROUBLESHOOTING

Porous Welds: Check if the gas cylinder is empty or if there is a draft blowing away the shielding gas.  
Wire Nesting (Bird Nest): Stop immediately. Check the drive roll tension and ensure the contact tip is not clogged or melted.
